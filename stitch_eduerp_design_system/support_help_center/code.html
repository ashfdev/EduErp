star Frequently Asked Questions

How do I bulk import student data? expand_more
Navigate to Students > Import. Download the CSV template, fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk), and upload the file. Ensure dates are in YYYY-MM-DD format.
Why aren't attendance notifications sending to parents? expand_more
Check if SMS/Email integration is configured in Settings > Integrations. Also, verify that the parent's contact information is complete and marked as 'Primary Contact' in the student's profile.
How do I lock grades for a completed term? expand_more
Go to Academics > Term Management. Select the term and click the 'Lock Grades' action button. This requires Administrator privileges and cannot be undone without contacting higher support.
